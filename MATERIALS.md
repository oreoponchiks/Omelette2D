# Additional materials

50 new materials, including bombs and explosives. Temperatures and reactions are game rules tuned for this sandbox.

| Material | Category | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salt | Powders | Dissolves in water to make salt water; recovered by boiling it. |
| Sugar | Powders | Dissolves into sugar water; burns to ash when heated. |
| Dust | Powders | Light powder that is easily carried by blast winds; burns quickly. |
| Ash | Powders | Light, nonflammable residue that drifts in air currents. |
| Coal | Powders | Dense fuel; ignites above 350 C and burns slowly to ash. |
| Snow | Powders | Light frozen powder; melts into water above 2 C. |
| Gravel | Powders | Heavy loose stone; melts into lava above 1050 C. |
| Clay | Powders | Water makes mud; firing above 650 C makes solid brick. |
| Soil | Powders | Supports seeds; water turns it into mud. |
| Sawdust | Powders | Light wood powder; ignites readily and leaves ash. |
| Rust | Powders | Dense oxidized powder; melts into molten metal above 1250 C. |
| Metal Dust | Powders | Heavy, conductive powder; melts above 1250 C. |
| Glass Shards | Powders | Loose glass fragments; fuse into molten glass above 1400 C. |
| Salt Water | Liquids | Denser than water; boiling leaves salt and emits steam. |
| Sugar Water | Liquids | Syrupy water; boiling leaves sugar and emits steam. |
| Honey | Liquids | Very viscous; mixes with water into sugar water and chars when hot. |
| Alcohol | Liquids | Fast-flowing fuel that ignites above 160 C. |
| Gasoline | Liquids | Light liquid fuel; ignites above 120 C with a pressure pulse. |
| Mercury | Liquids | Very dense, highly conductive liquid that sinks through other fluids. |
| Liquid Nitrogen | Liquids | Starts at -210 C; cools nearby matter, evaporating above -180 C into inert gas. |
| Molten Metal | Liquids | Conductive molten liquid; solidifies into metal below 1150 C. |
| Molten Glass | Liquids | Very viscous hot glass; solidifies below 1250 C. |
| Liquid Wax | Liquids | Slow-flowing wax; solidifies below 45 C and burns above 300 C. |
| Glass | Solids | Transparent wall; melts above 1400 C and shatters under pressure. |
| Metal | Solids | Conductive wall; melts above 1250 C and slowly rusts beside water. |
| Copper | Solids | Excellent heat conductor; melts into molten metal above 1080 C. |
| Steel | Solids | Strong conductive wall; melts above 1500 C and resists weaker blasts. |
| Brick | Solids | Heat-resistant wall; becomes gravel in strong blasts, lava above 1600 C. |
| Concrete | Solids | Heavy wall; fractures into gravel under high pressure or blasts. |
| Obsidian | Solids | Dark, heat-resistant wall; melts above 1500 C. |
| Rubber | Solids | Insulating solid; burns above 400 C and produces smoke. |
| Plastic | Solids | Insulating solid; burns above 250 C and produces smoke. |
| Wax | Solids | Melts into liquid wax above 60 C; can fuel a fire. |
| Hydrogen | Gases | Very buoyant fuel gas; heat above 180 C ignites a small blast. |
| Oxygen | Gases | Diffusing gas that is consumed near flames to intensify burning. |
| Carbon Dioxide | Gases | Heavy, settling gas that extinguishes adjacent fire. |
| Helium | Gases | Inert, fast-rising gas; also used as the inert cryogenic vapor. |
| Natural Gas | Gases | Buoyant fuel gas; ignites above 200 C with a small blast. |
| Dry Ice | Powders | Starts at -100 C; sublimes into carbon dioxide above -75 C. |
| Seed | Life | Beside soil or mud and water, germinates into a plant. |
| Moss | Life | Slowly consumes adjacent water to grow; burns above 180 C. |
| Algae | Life | Floating life that consumes water to spread; burns above 180 C. |
| Gunpowder | Explosives | Heat above 180 C triggers a small blast; chains through nearby explosives. |
| Fuse | Explosives | Ignites above 180 C; burns along neighboring fuse cells to trigger explosives. |
| TNT | Explosives | Stationary explosive; heat above 300 C or a nearby blast detonates it. |
| Timed Bomb | Explosives | Falls and detonates after 3 simulation seconds; flashes as its timer runs down. |
| Impact Bomb | Explosives | Falls and detonates on a hard impact, heat, or a nearby blast. |
| Remote Bomb | Explosives | Place charges, then press Detonate remote bombs. Heat or blasts also trigger them. |
| Fire Bomb | Explosives | After 3 simulation seconds, bursts into a hot cloud of fire. |
| Cryo Bomb | Explosives | After 3 simulation seconds, releases a freezing blast and cold vapor. |
